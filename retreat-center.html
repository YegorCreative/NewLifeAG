Retreat Center & Property

The mission of New Life Assembly of God Retreat Center is to provide a place where God’s people can be refreshed and renewed.

The Property

49 Acres to Rest and Grow

Our facility is located on 49 acres, has year-round overnight accommodations for up to 45, a full kitchen, hiking trails, gym, game room, and covered picnic area.

  • Full Kitchen
  • Hiking Trails
  • Gym & Game Room
  • Covered Picnic Area
  • Sleeps up to 45, year-round

Retreat Center

A Place to Gather, Rest & Renew

A look inside the New Life Conference and Retreat Center — lodging, gathering spaces, recreation, and the grounds where guests come to be refreshed.

Planning Your Stay

Cost & What to Bring

Cost: $15 per person per night. Groups pay for a minimum of 12 people.

You will need to bring: a sleeping bag or twin bedding, toiletries, and food.

Retreat Center Use Policy

Planning a Retreat or Event?

Groups using the New Life Conference and Retreat Center should review the official Use Policy before their stay. Download the document, complete it, sign and date it, then return the completed policy to New Life administration.

  1. 01

    Download

    Download the official Retreat Center Use Policy.

  2. 02

    Review

    Read the Statement of Faith, rules and regulations, guest information, and protection clause.

  3. 03

    Sign

    Complete the signature and date section at the end of the document.

  4. 04

    Return

    Send the completed signed document to New Life administration.
