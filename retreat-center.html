Retreat Center & Property

The mission of New Life Assembly of God Retreat Center is to provide a place where God’s people can be refreshed and renewed.

The Property

49 Acres to Rest and Grow

Our facility is located on 49 acres, has year-round overnight accommodations for up to 45, a full kitchen, hiking trails, gym, game room, and covered picnic area.

  • Full Kitchen
  • Hiking Trails
  • Gym & Game Room
  • Covered Picnic Area
  • Sleeps up to 45, year-round

Planning Your Stay

Cost & What to Bring

Cost: $15 per person per night. Groups pay for a minimum of 12 people.

You will need to bring: a sleeping bag or twin bedding, toiletries, and food.

Use Policy

Guidelines for Your Stay

Churches must agree to and sign our use policy. Highlights:

  • Standard check-in is 5:00 p.m.; check-out is 12:00 p.m. Arrangements outside these times must be made in advance with guest services.
  • No profane language, alcoholic beverages, illicit drugs, pornographic materials, or gambling. Smoking of any kind is not permitted in the buildings.
  • We believe that marriage is between one man and one woman. Therefore, we do not recognize gay marriage and prohibit homosexual behavior on the New Life Assembly of God grounds and facility. We also prohibit sexual activities between attendees, except those between a man and woman who are married to each other.
  • Firearms are prohibited unless the facility has been reserved for hunting or shooting, with specific approval and compliance with New York State game laws.
  • All vehicles must remain on parking lots and approved trails.
  • Signs, posters, banners, or pictures may not be hung without permission from the guest services office.
  • You may not cut down any trees and must respect the property by keeping it clean.
  • Campfires are permitted in the designated fire pit only.
  • All garbage must go in designated bins.
  • Guests are to be attired modestly at all times.
  • Footwear must be worn outdoors at all times.
  • No pets are allowed in the lodges. Pets are allowed on trails but must be picked up after by their owners.
  • Quiet time is 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.
