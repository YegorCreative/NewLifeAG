November

A special opportunity for fellowship and thanksgiving.

November 21, 2026

Community Thanksgiving Dinner

Gather with the New Life church family and our neighbors for a Thanksgiving dinner centered on fellowship and gratitude to God.

Why We Celebrate

Thanksgiving is an opportunity for us to gather as a church and community and remember the goodness and faithfulness of God. We give thanks not only for what God has provided, but for who He is. Around the table, we celebrate fellowship, generosity, and the opportunity to share God’s love with our neighbors.

“Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; his love endures forever.”

— Psalm 107:1 (NIV)

Registration Required

Please call to register for the Community Thanksgiving Dinner.

Looking Back

A Testimony of Thanksgiving
600+ Meals Served in 2025

Last year, New Life had the privilege of serving more than 600 meals through our Community Thanksgiving Dinner. This number is a testimony of God’s provision, of volunteers who gave their time, and of a church family glad to serve its community with gratitude and love.

“Serve one another humbly in love.”

— Galatians 5:13 (NIV)

December 25, 2026

Christmas

Why We Celebrate Christmas

Christmas is more than a season or tradition. We celebrate the birth of Jesus Christ and God’s gift of hope and salvation to the world. Christmas reminds us that God came near and that the greatest gift was given to us in Jesus.

“For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.”

— Luke 2:11 (KJV)
