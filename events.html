What's Happening

Events & Programs

Ongoing ministries, annual traditions, and ways to get involved at New Life.

Weekly

Every Week at New Life

  • Sunday Worship — 10:30 AM & 6:00 PM (the evening service has also been described as a Prayer Service)
  • Sunday School — 9:30 AM
  • Wednesday Bible Study — 7:00 PM
  • Live streaming on Rumble, YouTube, and Facebook every Sunday and Wednesday — see the Messages page
  • Sunday morning services are translated into Spanish and Chinese

Ongoing Programs

Ministries & Annual Traditions

Sports Camps

Soccer, Volleyball, Basketball, and Cheerleading camps, plus a Sportsman’s Dinner event.

Home School Co-op

Physical Education, Arts and Crafts on the 2nd and 4th Thursday of each month (holidays excepted).

Thanksgiving & Veterans Dinner

A free annual Thanksgiving Dinner and Veterans appreciation dinner. Watch the road sign for sign-up details.

Children & Youth Ministry

Pastor Mike & Debbie Perez minister to children through puppets and drama; Pastor Steve & Gwendolyn Murawski lead youth ministry. See Our Pastors.
