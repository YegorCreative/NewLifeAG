New Here?

Planning Your First Visit

We know visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here’s what to expect when you join us.

01

Arrive Anytime After 10:15

Our doors open ahead of the 10:30 AM service so you have time to find a seat and settle in.

02

Come As You Are

You’ll see everything from jeans to Sunday best. Wear what’s comfortable — we’re glad you’re here.

03

Families Welcome

Families are welcome to worship together at New Life.

04

Say Hello

Look for a greeter at the door or reach out beforehand — we’d love to meet you.

Our Story

Loving Jesus, Living New Life

Loving People, Extending Hope.

New Life Assembly of God is a Bible believing Christian church who holds to the truth that Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. We are a part of the Assemblies of God.

Oh how easy it is to get lost! With our cell phones and iPads, our 24-hour news cycles, there’s virtually nowhere to go for a moment of peace and quiet. That’s where New Life comes in — a holy place of respite in the tumult of life.
