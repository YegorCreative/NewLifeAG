New Here?

Planning Your First Visit

We know visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here's what to expect when you join us.

01

Arrive Anytime After 10:15

Our doors open ahead of the 10:30 AM service so you have time to find a seat and settle in.

02

Come As You Are

You'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best. Wear what's comfortable — we're glad you're here.

03

Families Welcome

Kids are always welcome in worship with us. Contact the church office ahead of time with any questions.

04

Say Hello

Look for a greeter at the door or reach out beforehand — we'd love to meet you.
